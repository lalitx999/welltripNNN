## 2. Cultural Background & 4 Ethnic Tribes

Sisaket Province is uniquely defined by its blend of 4 indigenous ethnic groups, each contributing distinct linguistic, culinary, and artisanal traditions:

1. **Khmer (เขมร)**: Concentrated in southern districts (Kantharalak, Khun Han, Phu Sing, Khukhan). Known for ancient Khmer stone temples, San Don Ta ancestor worship, and traditional silk weaving.
2. **Lao (ลาว)**: Concentrated along the Mun River basin (Mueang Sisaket, Kanthararom, Rasi Salai). Known for Mor Lam music, sticky rice culture, bamboo crafts, and river fish gastronomy.
3. **Suai / Kouy (ส่วย/กูย)**: Famous for elephant husbandry, deep forest knowledge, herbal medicine, and unique dialect traditions.
4. **Yoe (เยอ)**: Concentrated in Mueang Phrai Bueng (Ban Prasat Yoe). Known for distinctive dark indigo handwoven textiles, ancient temple archways, and Luang Pu Mum reverence.

- **Khun Han Volcano Robusta Coffee (กาแฟโรบัสต้าดินภูเขาไฟ GI)**: Grown on high-mineral volcanic soil in Khun Han District. 100% Robusta beans offering a rich, smooth flavor profile. Packaged in 500g bags.
- **Sisaket GI Shallots & Garlic (หอมแดงและกระเทียมโทน GI)**: Renowned throughout Thailand for tight bulbs, intense aroma, high allicin content, and long shelf life.

14. **วัดไพรพัฒนา (Wat Phrai Phatthana)** - Phu Sing District: Shrine of Luang Pu Suang (Teveda Len Din).

18. **วัดสุพรรณหงส์ (Wat Suphannahong)** - Mueang District: Temple chapel built on a simulated Suphannahong royal barge floating on water.
19. **วัดป่ามหาเจดีย์แก้ว / วัดล้านขวด (Wat Pa Maha Chedi Kaew)** - Khun Han District: Globally famous temple constructed with over 1.5 million recycled glass bottles.

### Historical Castles & Monuments (9)
23. **ปราสาทสระกำแพงใหญ่ (Prasat Sa Kamphaeng Yai)** - Uthumphon Phisai District: Largest Khmer sandstone castle in Sisaket (11th Century Baphuon style).

26. **ปราสาทเมืองจันทร์ (Prasat Mueang Chan)** - Mueang Chan District: Ancient moated Khmer sanctuary ruins.
27. **ปราสาทตาเล็ง (Prasat Ta Leng)** - Khukhan District: Single sandstone tower with intact Kleang-style lintels.
28. **ปราสาทบ้านปราสาท (Prasat Ban Prasat)** - Huai Thap Than District: Trio of brick towers located near a 2,000-year-old archaeological site.
29. **ปราสาทบ้านสมอ (Prasat Ban Samo)** - Prang Ku District: Ancient Khmer Arokayasala chapel structure.
30. **ปราสาทปรางค์กู่ (Prasat Prang Ku)** - Prang Ku District: Laterite Khmer sanctuary adjacent to a large ancient Baray reservoir.
31. **สถูปคู่ (Sathup Khu)** - Kantharalak District: Twin sandstone stupas located on Pha Mo E-Daeng cliff.

## 8. API Architecture & Developer Integration

The WellTrip platform provides RESTful API endpoints for external integrations:

- `POST /api/v1/auth/login/`: User & Admin Authentication (JWT Access + Refresh)
- `POST /api/v1/auth/oauth/google/`: Google OAuth 2.0 ID Token verification
- `GET /api/v1/accommodations/`: Lodging & homestay directory with room calendar availability
- `GET /api/v1/otop/`: OTOP product catalog with inventory management
- `GET /api/v1/services/foods/`: Healthy local dining & calorie-tracked menus
- `GET /api/v1/services/wellness/`: Herbal spa packages and session slot booking
- `POST /api/v1/payments/verify-slip/`: PromptPay slip verification via EasySlip API
