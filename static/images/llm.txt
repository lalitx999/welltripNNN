# WellTrip Sisaket - Smart Eco-Wellness Tourism Platform

> WellTrip Sisaket is the official smart eco-wellness tourism platform for Sisaket Province, Thailand. It connects tourists with community homestays, local organic dining, herbal wellness spas, GI volcano products, historical Khmer castles, and community events.

## Core Factual Information
- **Location**: Sisaket Province, Northeastern Thailand (Isan).
- **Cultural Identity**: Land of 4 Ethnic Tribes (Khmer, Lao, Suai/Kouy, Yoe).
- **Key Attractions**: 31 official tourist destinations including Pha Mo E-Daeng cliff, Prasat Sa Kamphaeng Yai castle, Wat Pa Maha Chedi Kaew (Wat Lan Khuat - 1.5 million bottle temple), Wat Phrai Phatthana (Luang Pu Suang), Suan Somdet Srinagarindra (40,000 Lamduan trees), and Sisaket Aquarium.
- **GI Products**: Sisaket Lava Durian (ทุเรียนภูเขาไฟ GI), Khun Han Volcano Robusta Coffee 500g (กาแฟโรบัสต้าดินภูเขาไฟ GI), Sisaket Shallots & Garlic GI.
- **Key Events**: Sound of Sisaket 2026, 244th Sisaket City Celebration, Lava Durian Fest, Lamduan Flower Festival, San Don Ta Khmer Ancestor Festival.
